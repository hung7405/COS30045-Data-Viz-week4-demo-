    How the data was prepared

    Audience and story

    Australian shoppers comparing TVs. The story runs from market overview, to sizes, brands and a fair power comparison, to one buying rule. Every chapter follows question, chart, so what.

    Cleaning steps

    • Brands upper-cased; SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS merged into SAMSUNG; Q.BELL into QBELL; S VISION into SVISION (74 brands).
    • Kept only Available models whose SoldIn contains Australia.
    • Inches = screensize ÷ 2.54. Size class: small under 43″, medium 43–65″, large above 65″.
    • Standby: - read as missing (17.7% of models), never as zero.

    Size-fair index

    To compare brands and technologies without size getting in the way, each model's power is divided by the median power of TVs within 2 inches of its size, then multiplied by 100. A score of 100 is typical for that size; 80 uses 20% less.

    Accuracy, privacy and ethics

    A catalogue snapshot: there are no sales, prices or dates sold, so shelf share is not market share and nothing here is a trend. Product data only, no personal information. Charts show association, not causation. Groups under 30 models (small OLED, n=17) are flagged, not ranked. No brand is promoted.

    Technology

    HTML, CSS and JavaScript with D3.js v7 for 2D charts. Depth effects are CSS 3D transforms on the page, never on the charts: 3D charts hide values behind perspective, so every chart here is flat and labelled.
